About Me

I am a Mechanical Engineer and BIM MEP Modeler with 3+ years of experience in HVAC design, Revit MEP modelling and MEP coordination.

I specialise in developing HVAC models up to LOD 350, working with chilled-water, AHU, FCU, VRF and DX systems. My experience covers Carrier HAP load calculations, HVAC shop drawings and Navisworks model coordination, in line with ASHRAE, SMACNA and the Saudi Building Code (SBC).

I enjoy combining engineering knowledge with BIM technology to deliver accurate, coordinated and practical building solutions, from the model to the site.
